RyadaFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

RyadaFit ("the app") is an AI-powered fitness coaching app operated by Firas Dev Studio ("we"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your choices. Contact: firasmzoughi2001@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), or your Google / Apple account identifier and email if you sign in with those.
  • Profile and fitness data you enter: name, age, sex, height, weight, goals, sports, training schedule, workouts, sets, reps, weights, body measurements, food entries and progress entries.
  • Photos: a profile picture, only if you choose one from your gallery or camera.
  • Voice and files: audio you record for voice-to-text and PDF files you attach in the AI coach chat, only when you use those features.
  • AI coach messages: the messages you send to the coach and its replies.
  • Health data (optional): if you turn on Apple Health or Health Connect sync, the activity data you allow.

2. How we use it

To create your account, build and adapt your training plans, log and show your progress, power the AI coach, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google / Apple provide sign-in when you choose it.
  • AI providers: your coach messages, voice transcripts, attached PDFs and the profile details needed for a plan are sent through our server to an AI model provider (OpenRouter and the model it routes to) to generate replies and plans.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with row-level access controls so each user can read only their own data. No system is perfectly secure.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and all associated data, email firasmzoughi2001@gmail.com from the address you registered with. We delete it within 30 days. Backups may persist for a short additional period.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR) you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data-protection authority.

7. Children

RyadaFit is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Medical disclaimer

RyadaFit provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical service. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise programme.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and significant changes will be noted in the app.
